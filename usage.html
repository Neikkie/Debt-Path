How to Use Debt Path

From your first balance to your final payment.

1. Add Your Debts

Start by entering each debt you want Debt Path to include in your payoff calculations.

You may be asked to provide information such as:

  • Debt name and type
  • Current balance
  • Minimum monthly payment
  • Annual percentage rate (APR)
  • Payment due date

2. Review Your Dashboard

Your Dashboard provides an overview of the debts you entered and estimated information about your payoff plan.

Depending on your information, Debt Path may display your total debt, monthly payments, estimated interest, payoff progress, focus debt, and estimated debt-free timeline.

3. Select a Payoff Strategy

Debt Path lets you compare different approaches to paying down debt.

  • Avalanche: prioritizes debts with the highest interest rates.
  • Snowball: prioritizes debts with the smallest balances.
  • Custom: allows you to choose your own debt priority.

4. Add Extra Payments

If your budget allows, you can add recurring or one-time extra payments to your plan.

Debt Path recalculates your estimated payoff schedule so you can see how the additional payment could affect your timeline.

5. Try the Simulator

The Simulator allows you to explore hypothetical scenarios without changing your primary plan.

You can experiment with changes such as:

  • Extra monthly payments
  • One-time payments
  • Interest-rate changes
  • Different plan start dates

6. Log Payments

Record payments as you make them to keep your progress information current.

7. Use Payment Reminders

If enabled, Debt Path can provide optional reminders based on the payment due dates you enter.

Important: Debt Path calculations are estimates. Actual interest, lender calculations, fees, payment allocation, and payoff dates may differ. Always verify amounts with your lender or creditor.
