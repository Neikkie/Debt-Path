Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

Chaniiapps LLC ("Chaniiapps," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use Debt Path.

Information You Enter

Debt Path may allow you to enter information related to your debts and payments, including debt names, balances, interest rates, minimum payments, due dates, and payment activity.

This information is used to provide the application's debt tracking, payoff planning, simulation, and progress features.

Financial Account Access

Debt Path is a debt planning and tracking application. Unless a feature expressly states otherwise, the app does not require direct access to your bank account, credit card account, or lender account in order to create payoff estimates.

iCloud

If Debt Path offers an optional iCloud backup feature and you choose to enable it, applicable app information may be stored using Apple's iCloud services associated with your Apple account.

Your use of iCloud is also subject to Apple's applicable terms, privacy policies, and security practices.

Notifications

If you enable payment reminders, Debt Path may use notification permissions on your device to provide reminders related to the due dates you enter.

You can change notification permissions through your device settings.

Data We May Receive Automatically

Depending on the technologies and services used by the app, limited technical information may be generated as part of normal app operation, diagnostics, crash reporting, or App Store services.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide Debt Path features and calculations
  • Maintain and improve app functionality
  • Identify technical issues
  • Provide requested reminders or device features
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

Sale of Personal Information

Chaniiapps LLC does not sell your personal information.

Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect information associated with the app. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Debt Path is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, applicable law, or our practices change. The effective date shown above will identify the latest version.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be submitted through the official Chaniiapps support contact provided with Debt Path or on the Chaniiapps website.
